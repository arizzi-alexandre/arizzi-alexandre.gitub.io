## Amazon Aurora

- Postgre and Mysql are compatible with aurora.
- CLaiming high performance and "Cloud native"
- Scaling from 10 GB to 256 TB and failover is **native**

### High availability and scaling
- Spreaded accross 3 AZ.
- One master and up to 15 aurora read replicas

The master is the only one to write on your volume. If the client want to access to one of the replica, as they automatically scale, they are all pointing to a Load balancer called Reader Endpoint.

![alt text](https://arizzi-alexandre.github.io/arizzi-alexandre.gitub.io/assets/image3.png)


- Custom endpoint: You define your own group of instances for example: heavy isolated operations, mixed instances size
